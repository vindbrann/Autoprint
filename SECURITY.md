## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please do **not** open a public issue for security problems.

Instead, use GitHub's private reporting: go to the **Security** tab of this
repository and click **Report a vulnerability**.

Please include:
- a description of the problem and its potential impact
- the affected version
- steps to reproduce it

Autoprint is maintained by a single person in their spare time, so there is
no guaranteed response time. Reports are handled on a best-effort basis, and
I will do my best to acknowledge them and fix confirmed vulnerabilities as
quickly as my availability allows. If a report is accepted, a fix and a
public advisory will be published, with credit to the reporter if desired.
